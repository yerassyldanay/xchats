# Concept

The product is a collaborative messaging workspace for businesses that talk to customers through WhatsApp first, and later through other social channels.

It is not a classic chatbot. The core idea is to wrap social messengers with a shared team inbox where humans stay in control, while the AI assistant suggests useful replies, media files, summaries, and next actions. The assistant can help agents respond faster, but the default experience is suggestion and approval, not uncontrolled auto-response.

The first supported channel is WhatsApp through Evolution API. The platform should later support other channels such as Instagram, Telegram, Facebook Messenger, website chat, or email without changing the core conversation model.

## Vision

Businesses should be able to connect one or many WhatsApp accounts, invite members, receive customer messages in real time, assign conversations, and respond with help from AI. The AI should understand conversation context, business knowledge, and available media assets.

The product should feel like a lightweight Chatwoot-style team inbox, but without the full complexity of a generic helpdesk platform.

## Core Principles

- WhatsApp is the first channel, not the only future channel.
- Humans control customer communication by default.
- AI suggests text, media, summaries, and actions.
- Multiple members can work from the same shared inbox.
- Multiple WhatsApp accounts can belong to one organization.
- The app owns normalized contacts, conversations, messages, assignment, AI state, and media references.
- Evolution API is treated as the WhatsApp transport, not as the product database.

## Glossary

**Organization**  
The company or workspace using the product.

**Member**  
A user inside an organization. In the first version, all members have equal access.

**Channel**  
A communication source such as WhatsApp, Instagram, Telegram, or website chat.

**WhatsApp Account**  
One connected WhatsApp number/instance managed through Evolution API.

**Evolution API**  
The external WhatsApp gateway used to connect WhatsApp accounts, receive events, and send messages.

**Contact**  
The customer or external person talking to the business. A contact may have several identities, such as phone number, WhatsApp JID, or LID JID.

**Conversation**  
A chat thread between one WhatsApp account and one contact. It can be open, assigned, resolved, or waiting.

**Message**  
An inbound or outbound item inside a conversation. It can be text, image, video, audio, document, sticker, or system event.

**Assignment**  
The member currently responsible for a conversation.

**AI Assistant**  
The component that reads conversation context and suggests replies, summaries, media, and next actions.

**AI Draft**  
A suggested response generated by AI but not yet sent to the customer.

**Media Asset**  
A reusable file, such as a product photo, payment guide, PDF, voice note, or video, that can be suggested or sent in a conversation.

**Raw Event**  
The original webhook payload received from Evolution API before normalization.
